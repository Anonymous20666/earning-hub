## Scope implemented

- Real invite metadata: decode nested amp-escaped decimal and hexadecimal entities, including astral Unicode; remove decoded tags, invalid code points and control characters. HTTPS/public-DNS/IP pinning, redirect/body limits and image validation remain intact. This is metadata evidence, not a physical WhatsApp rendering claim. The invite token is not recorded in the new source/tests/evidence.
- Personal composer now uses native membership and posting rights, not a mandatory admin-command handshake. Status is attempted even where ordinary chat is admin-only. DOUBLE sends status first and explicitly skips a locked chat, rather than rejecting both. Late locks, unavailable membership, owner ignores/exclusions and removal bans remain enforced at send. New native paid reservations follow the same rights, retain explicit worker paid opt-in/funding/receipt gates, and reserve no reward for pre-skipped locked chat legs. A versioned immutable policy marker grandfathers older queued runs under their original consent contract. Legacy direct personal API remains consent-bound; owners use a fresh explicit composer review for the new behavior.
- Owned WhatsApp number 1 / number 2 / both per personal post. Selection is validated before scanning, persisted in the ten-minute immutable review, rechecked at submission and bound to targets. There is no silent fallback to an unselected number. Shared groups receive one copy per surface; one existing owner wallet remains shared.
- Searchable, deduplicated owned-group ignore checkboxes, with persistent checked-state and rollback/refresh handling. Original repeated invite-link input remains, with metadata-only ownership checks and no autojoin. Ignore changes invalidate a prepared review and are checked again immediately before a queued send.
- New pairing requests pass **PAPPYPAY** as the actual SDK custom pairing-code argument; unexpected provider output fails instead of presenting invented branding as a successful code. The UI waits for provider readiness. Existing sessions are not unlinked or re-paired.
- Simple earning-first homepage and dashboard; free personal posts and advertiser planning remain secondary, clearly separate actions. Stronger visible buttons; coherent warm-green/cream scene materials, gold Coin geometry and depth. Existing automatic Android motion, visibility pause, explicit motion preference and reduced-motion behavior remain.
- Separate public `/#/earnings`, `/#/coin-value` and `/#/calculator` pages; exact rates, conditions and cashout thresholds are off the landing page. Calculator imports deduplicated owned group counts and supports both 1,000/day and 5,000/day theoretical workloads. Currency display leads with USD, then NGN and ten selectable other currencies. ExchangeRate-API's 15 October 2026 UTC snapshot is clearly dated, attributed, display-only and not a live FX quote or payout promise.
- Console distinguishes WhatsApp acceptance from verified delivery, group skips, skipped post types, failures and uncertain sends. Only the installed SDK's authoritative numeric Boom rejections are classified as failed after send begins; generic/network/timeout errors remain UNKNOWN and never automatically retry.

## Economics and safety preserved

Founder confirmed **keep the existing reward formula**: 100 members = 0.100 Coins; 1,000+ = 1 Coin per confirmed funded post type; under 100 = zero. Internal backing remains 1 Coin = ₦1, not US$1. Referral remains 100 Coins once after qualifying funded work. No personal reward, paid-campaign participation or referral qualification is generated by personal posting. No paid dispatch, payment or payout gate is enabled. Pairing does not create income. Native status support/rejections and actual rendering still depend on WhatsApp; no physical-device send or fresh real pairing was attempted by the agent.

1,000/day illustration: 50 distinct 1,000+ member groups × 10 funded campaigns × 2 independently confirmed post types. 5,000/day illustration: 250 such groups × 10 × 2. Availability, funding, posting rights, actual success and verification are mandatory; these are not typical earnings or guaranteed work. Paid campaigns have no 100-group ceiling. At v0.8.0 the personal beta still had a 200-group/run ceiling, three runs/UTC day and a one-minute gap. These personal quotas were explicitly revoked by the founder and removed in v0.9.0; see `MOBILE-POLISH-0.9.0.md`.
